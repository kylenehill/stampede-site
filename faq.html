Age Restrictions

Everyone is invited every day we are open! On Fridays and Saturdays, guests must be 21 or older after 8 PM when our live bands play, as we are then considered a nightclub with 21+ age limits.

Pets

We're big animal lovers around here, but due to health rules, we can't have pets at Stampede. We wish we could! Service animals, of course, are always welcome.

Entertainment

We have an event every day we're open, and live music every Friday and Saturday night! We mainly feature country bands, but per request we're mixing it up with some classic rock too. Watch our social media for the latest on who's playing each weekend!

Hiring & Booking

Email your resume or booking inquiry to info@stampedelivermore.com and we'll be in touch!

Dietary Options

Absolutely! We have options for all dietary needs. Just ask your server for recommendations and we'll take care of you.

Reservations

We highly encourage reservations — we get very busy on dance nights! Call us at (925) 200-8783 and we can book a table up to 6:30 PM on Friday/Saturday. You can also email a few days in advance at stampede.livermore@gmail.com.

Parties & Celebrations

Yes! We are the ultimate party venue. With dancing until dawn, live music, and bull riding — it's a night no one forgets. Reach out at (925) 200-8783 to learn more about booking The Boar's Nest private saloon!
